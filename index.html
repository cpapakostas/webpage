Academic Profile

Religion • Informatics • Technology

I am an interdisciplinary scholar working at the intersection of Religion, Informatics, and Technology. My research focuses on the application and critical study of Artificial Intelligence, Extended Reality, adaptive learning systems, and digital technologies in Religious Education, Digital Theology, and religious and cultural heritage.

My academic background combines Informatics and Engineering with Theology and Religious Studies. I hold a Ph.D. in Software and Information Systems from the University of West Attica, where my doctoral research focused on mobile Augmented Reality, fuzzy logic, learner modelling, and personalized learning. I also hold a B.Sc. in Social Theology and Religious Studies from the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens, together with a Dipl.Eng. and an M.Sc. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Democritus University of Thrace.

My current work extends computational and educational approaches to religious contexts, including AI-supported theological education, immersive learning, digital religious heritage, religious inclusion, interfaith dialogue, and the historical and geopolitical dimensions of religion. A central strand of my research concerns responsible and human-centered AI, with emphasis on transparency, explainability, value-sensitive design, theological reliability, and human oversight.

Research Areas

01

AI & Adaptive Learning in Religious Education

Design and evaluation of intelligent and adaptive learning environments, with emphasis on learner modelling, personalization, fuzzy logic, learning analytics, knowledge tracing, and AI-supported tutoring in educational and religious contexts.

02

Digital Theology & Human-Centered AI

Responsible use of AI in theology and Religious Education, focusing on transparency, explainability, value-sensitive design, theological reliability, bias, accountability, calibrated trust, and meaningful human oversight.

03

Extended Reality & Religious Heritage

Augmented, Virtual, and Mixed Reality for immersive learning, religious and cultural heritage, sacred spaces, monuments, digital interpretation, user experience, and personalized interaction with historical and cultural content.

04

Religion, Society & Geopolitics

Digital approaches to religious diversity, inclusion, interfaith dialogue, cultural adaptation, religious diplomacy, and the historical, social, and geopolitical dimensions of religion.
